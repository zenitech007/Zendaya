What is "the orb" in this spec?

The current orb is a procedural sphere with a glow shader. No mesh, no rig, no mouth, no body. Visemes & body-action need an interpretation. Pick one.

●

A. Keep procedural orb, reinterpret channels (Recommended)

amplitude → scale (existing, fix broadcast).
visemes → ripple/distortion strength (orb "talks" but no literal lips).
body action → cute discrete GSAP keyframes: nod=vertical bounce, shake=wobble, wave=orbital arc, shrug=expand-and-relax.
perception/telemetry → separate widgets, not on the orb.

Honest scope. Ships fast. Respects current build.
◍

B. Richer orb mesh (mouth-on-sphere)

Replace procedural sphere with a low-poly stylised face/mouth mesh. Real viseme blendshapes drive a literal mouth. Body action still abstract.

~3× the scope. Needs a designed mesh + morph targets
🧍

C. Swap to VRM avatar (like zendaya-pet)

Replace the orb entirely with a VRM humanoid. Real bones for wave/shrug, real blendshapes for visemes. Best for "human assistant" vibe.

Biggest rewrite. Different product direction
