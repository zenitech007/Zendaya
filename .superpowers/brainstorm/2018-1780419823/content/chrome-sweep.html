Chrome — how does the frame react to a scene change?

The SVG ring (Forge) / aperture (Iris) frame, coupled to the same 1.2s morph. Each tile is looping the behavior. Click one.

1 — Aperture pulse  (recommended)

On scene change the rings contract inward then reopen, like a camera iris blinking, timed to the morph — the frame physically reacts as the orb becomes globe/weather/clock. (Can add a faint brightness flare on top.)

2 — Spin-up + flash

The rings keep rotating but briefly accelerate and flare brighter during the morph, then settle back to idle. Subtle — an energy surge rather than a structural move.

3 — Radar sweep

A bright arc sweeps once around the frame on each scene change (like a radar/scan line), lighting tick marks as it passes. Signals “reconfiguring” — more decorative than structural.
