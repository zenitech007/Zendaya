WeatherScene — what is the hologram made of?

All three react to the real condition (sun / cloud / rain / storm) and tint to the active theme. The difference is the rendering language. Click one.

24°  PARTLY CLOUDY

1 — Particle morph  (recommended)

The orb’s 9,000 particles rearrange into the weather: a radiant sun-disc, drifting cloud volumes, streaming rain, turbulent storm. Reuses the exact Phase B dissolve engine — the morph is the moment.

2 — Volumetric sky-sphere

A glowing planet-like sphere with shader clouds swirling across its surface, sun-glow terminator, rain as a shimmer overlay. Reads as “the weather on a little world.” New shader work, less tied to the particle system.

3 — Line-art instrument

A clean holographic diagram: glowing line glyphs (sun, cloud, rain strokes) framed by instrument rings — closer to a JARVIS readout than a literal sky. Crisp and legible, less atmospheric/volumetric.
